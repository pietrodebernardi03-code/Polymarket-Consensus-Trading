\begin{abstract}
We test, out of sample and under a frozen pre-registration, whether copying
``smart-money'' Polymarket traders --- entering a market when $\geq 2$
point-in-time-qualified wallets independently hold the same outcome near their
entry price, and exiting when they exit or the market resolves --- earns a
positive, calibration-beating return. On the sample accrued to date the frozen
rule \prov{passes} both pre-registered success conditions (median per-trade
return \prov{$+44\%$}, hit-rate \prov{$74\%$} $>$ average entry price
\prov{$0.54$}). We stress that this reading is provisional and regime-narrow:
\prov{$\sim$90\%} of scored trades are sports resolving in a single two-week
window dominated by Wimbledon and the FIFA World Cup, and the non-sports stratum
is currently too small to interpret. Tracking continues to a pre-committed
evaluation point to test the rule across regimes.
\footnotetext{$\dagger$ Provisional value (22 Jul 2026, post-fix); to be
regenerated at the final evaluation point.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Hypothesis and Pre-Registration}
The strategy, universe, entry/exit rules, sizing, and success criterion were
frozen before any forward data were observed (see \texttt{PRE\_REGISTRATION.md},
v1.1, 5 July 2026). The pre-committed success criterion (\S6) is: after $\geq 30$
resolved paper trades, declare \textbf{success} iff the \emph{median} per-trade
return $>0$ \textbf{and} the hit-rate $>$ the average entry price paid.

\paragraph{Entry.} A \texttt{(market, outcome)} is opened when: $\geq 2$ qualified
roster wallets co-hold the outcome; price $\leq$ their median entry $+\,0.06$ and
within $[0.10, 0.90]$; $>48$h to resolution; no dissent (no other outcome of the
market has $\geq 2$ qualified holders); not previously booked.

\paragraph{Exit.} On the first of: qualified holders drop below 2 (copy-the-exit),
or resolution (settle 1/0).

\section{Data and Method}
Public Polymarket Data/Gamma APIs; no real capital. Roster re-qualified monthly
by a point-in-time gate ($\geq 30$ resolved trades, trailing win-rate $\geq 0.55$).
Per-trade return $r = (\text{exit}-\text{entry})/\text{entry}$. The scored sample
is trades with \texttt{exit\_type} $\in$ \{resolved\_win, resolved\_loss\} held
$\geq 48$h; a broader lens additionally includes copy-the-exit (\texttt{sharps\_left})
closes.

\subsection{Measurement correction (disclosed)}
\label{sec:fix}
A data-retrieval defect (v1.2, 23 July 2026) caused resolved markets to drop out
of the Gamma query, freezing the affected trades \texttt{OPEN}. The failure was
\emph{asymmetric}: losing outcomes pin to $\sim$0 and were usually recorded, while
winning outcomes were orphaned, biasing the interim read \emph{downward}. The fix
is retrieval-only (query \texttt{closed=true} first); no frozen rule changed.
Orphaned trades were back-filled with identical resolution semantics, applied to
all of them (winners and losers), verified against raw settlement prices, logged
to \texttt{resolve\_stuck\_audit.csv}, with the pre-fix ledger retained. Both
pre- and post-fix results are reported (Table~\ref{tab:strata}).

\section{Results (provisional)}
\label{sec:results}
On the scored sample ($n=\prov{42}$): median $=\prov{+0.44}$, mean
$=\prov{+0.52}$, hit-rate $=\prov{0.74}$ (\prov{31/42}), average entry price
$=\prov{0.54}$. Both success conditions are \prov{met}. The broader lens
($n=\prov{56}$, incl.\ copy-the-exit) gives median $=\prov{+0.30}$,
hit-rate $=\prov{0.70}$ --- same qualitative conclusion.

\section{Limitations}
(i) \textbf{Regime concentration:} single-window, sports-dominated sample;
the World Cup is a plausible outlier. (ii) \textbf{Survivorship (partial):} the
roster starts from today's leaderboard; forward testing reframes but does not
fully remove pool survivorship (see pre-registration \S8). (iii)
\textbf{Longshot skew:} returns are right-skewed; we therefore lead with the
median and hit-rate, never the mean alone. (iv) \textbf{Measurement correction:}
results depend on the \S\ref{sec:fix} back-fill; both ledgers are retained for
independent re-derivation. (v) \textbf{Provisional:} per pre-registration v1.3,
no verdict is final before the pre-committed evaluation point.
